Overview

This document sets out the visual identity of Xotica Badboy Kebabs. Use it whenever you create anything that carries our name — signage, packaging, menus, social posts or the website. It covers:

  • Logo
  • Font Suite
  • Colour Palette
  • Usage Guidelines

A brand is built over time, not in a single moment. It's the sum of every box we hand over, every sign we light up and every post we publish. Using our logo, colours and type consistently makes Xotica instantly recognisable and builds the trust that keeps customers coming back for their favourite flavour.

The elements in this guide are essential and should never be compromised. If you're ever unsure, come back to these pages before you publish.

Primary Logo

The Star

Our hero mark. It signals star quality and sits at the peak of the badge.

The Badge

The double-lined diamond frames the brand like a street-food stamp of approval.

BADBOY

The hand-drawn wordmark carries our attitude — loud, playful and never boring.

Xotica / Kebabs

Clean, spaced capitals that anchor the name and tell people exactly what we do.

Our primary logo is full colour: Badboy Red with Kebab Amber. Red speaks of heat, appetite and energy; amber brings warmth and that glowing, freshly-grilled feel. Use the full-colour logo on white or light backgrounds wherever possible.

Logo Variations

When full colour isn't possible, choose the version with the strongest contrast against the background.

Clear Space & Sizing

Clear space keeps the logo visible and uncluttered. The minimum clear space on every side is x, where x equals the height of the star. Keep text, images, edges and other graphics outside this area.
